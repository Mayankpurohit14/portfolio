What I actually do

I'm a full-stack developer with four-plus years of production experience, most of it spent architecting REST APIs in Laravel for a live space-and-service marketplace. That means booking and availability logic that can't double-book a room, payment flows through Razorpay and Stripe that can't lose a transaction, and payout systems hosts actually trust.

Outside the API layer, I build and maintain WordPress and WooCommerce sites for clients across healthcare, e-commerce, and the arts — usually the person who gets called when a site needs to go from "looks fine" to "actually works." I use Claude, Gemini, and DeepSeek daily to move faster through debugging and documentation, not to skip understanding what the code does.
